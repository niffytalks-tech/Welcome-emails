3 tips for the PRINCE2 Foundation exam
1. Memorise the 7 principles first

Every theme and process in PRINCE2 traces back to a principle. If you can recall all 7 cold — Continued Business Justification, Learn from Experience, Defined Roles, Manage by Stages, Manage by Exception, Focus on Products, Tailor to Suit — the rest of the exam becomes pattern recognition.

2. Know who does what in the project team

Organisation theme questions are some of the most common in the Foundation exam. Be clear on the difference between the Executive, Senior User, and Senior Supplier — and remember that Project Assurance cannot be delegated to the Project Manager.

3. Map every management product to its process

The exam loves asking "in which process is X produced?" Focus on: PID → Initiating, Project Brief → Starting Up, Stage Plan → Managing a Stage Boundary, Exception Report → Controlling a Stage. Get those anchored and you'll pick up marks quickly.

📊 The Foundation pass mark

The PRINCE2 Foundation exam is 60 questions in 60 minutes — you need 33 out of 60 (55%) to pass. Consistently scoring 12/15 or above in the quiz means you're in good shape. Aim for 13+ before you book the real thing.
